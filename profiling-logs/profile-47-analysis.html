Findings
🔴 frame.total always over budget
Every measured window exceeds 16.667ms. Best: 101.1% (menu), worst: 118.0% (frame 7200). Total = cpu_work + vsync-present. When cpu_work rises, present time shrinks (GPU gets work), but the sum still clips over budget, resulting in consistent ~1–3ms frame overruns.
🔴 gameplay.music — pathological spikes
Window avg hit 4.11ms @ frame 7200 (25% of budget!). Single-frame max: 36.69ms. P95 hits 15ms+ during active gameplay. At frames 1320–1560 it was the primary frame-blow cause alongside audio.route_step (max: 21.9ms). Deserves immediate investigation.
🔴 audio.route.removed_enemy_match — 6–12ms stalls
Fires each time an enemy leaves audio routing. Single call max: 12.45ms. At frame 7200 the 120-frame window avg was 2.47ms from these events alone. Appears O(n) or worse — a linear scan across all tracked routes. Peaks at ~40 total calls recorded across session.
🟡 system.ai_update scales with enemy count
0.6ms @ 9 enemies → 4.8ms @ 116 enemies (~0.041ms/enemy). Mostly linear. At max theoretical 200 enemies → ~8ms, pushing fixed_step near its 9.5ms budget. Current plateau of ~104 enemies keeps it at ~3.2ms steady state. Acceptable, but monitor if enemy cap rises.
🟡 system.physics_collision — 14.7ms spike
Window avg is fine (0.1–0.2ms) but max=14.733ms appears repeatedly after frame ~1320. Isolated worst-case collision resolution path. Not causing sustained overruns but would be a frame-stall when hit during an already-heavy frame.
🟡 frame.fixed_step max=96.6ms (init stutter)
Recorded from session start, persists in history readouts. Almost certainly a single-frame stall during world/flow-field init at frame ~300. Ongoing P95 is 4–11ms — fine. The 96ms max inflates "max" columns across all game.update subsystems but is not recurring.
🟢 frame.render well within budget
Stable 4.8–5.4ms vs 6.5ms budget. render.world: ~2.5ms, render.hud: ~0.85ms, render.world.maze: ~1.0ms, render.world.effects: ~0.5ms. No render regression across 13,200 frames. ~1ms of headroom vs budget even at peak enemy counts.
🟢 Memory: no leaks detected
Balanced alloc/free throughout (e.g. +109/-109 per window). Live heap: ~400KB during gameplay, returns to ~20KB on menu exit (frame 13200). Peak: 460KB. No growing unfreed allocation delta observed over the 220-second session.
